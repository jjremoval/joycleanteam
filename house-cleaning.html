Deep Cleaning for Fresh Starts & Hidden Spaces

Deep cleaning tackles the detailed work that regular cleanings skip. Perfect for seasonal refreshes, preparation for guests or events, or simply starting fresh.

Our deep cleaning includes baseboards, inside cabinets, behind and under appliances, light fixtures, door frames, and other often-overlooked areas that accumulate dust and grime.

Deep Cleaning Includes:

  • All standard cleaning tasks plus:
  • Baseboards and trim throughout the home
  • Inside kitchen cabinets, drawers, and pantry
  • Behind and under all appliances (refrigerator, stove, washer/dryer)
  • Windows, sills, and frames (interior)
  • Light fixtures, ceiling fans, and vents
  • Door frames and handles
  • Grout and tile detail work
  • Detailed organization and decluttering support

House Cleaning FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About House Cleaning

What is included in standard house cleaning?

Standard house cleaning includes dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom cleaning, trash removal, and tidying up common areas. We customize the scope based on your home's layout and your specific needs.

What's the difference between standard cleaning and deep cleaning?

Standard cleaning focuses on high-touch areas and regular maintenance. Deep cleaning includes baseboards, inside cabinets, behind appliances, light fixtures, and detail work that regular cleanings skip. Deep cleans are ideal 2-4 times per year.

How often should I have my house cleaned?

Most homeowners schedule weekly or biweekly cleaning. Frequency depends on your household size, activity level, and preferences. We offer flexible recurring schedules: weekly, biweekly, monthly, or as-needed.

Do you offer move-in and move-out cleaning?

Yes. We specialize in thorough move-in/move-out cleaning to help you get your deposit back or start fresh in a new home. This includes deep cleaning of every room, appliances, and fixtures.

Are you available for last-minute or same-day cleaning?

We try to accommodate last-minute requests when our schedule allows. Call us at (805) 457-4584 to check availability. Advance booking is always recommended to guarantee your preferred time.

What products do you use for cleaning?

We use professional-grade, effective cleaning products. If you prefer eco-friendly or specific products, let us know when scheduling and we can accommodate your preferences.

How much does house cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on your home's size, condition, service type, and frequency. We provide personalized free quotes so you know the total cost upfront with no hidden fees.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

It depends on your preference. Many clients provide a key code or leave access instructions so the team can work independently. We're happy to discuss your comfort level when scheduling.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Joy Clean Team is fully licensed and insured, protecting your home and providing peace of mind during every cleaning.

How do I book a house cleaning appointment?

Call us at (805) 457-4584 or request a free quote through our website. We'll discuss your cleaning needs, answer any questions, and schedule a time that works for you.
