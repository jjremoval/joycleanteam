Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I redeem a gift card?
Simply present your gift card to our clean team on your cleaning day. If you won't be present, just leave the gift card in an envelope for the team to find.
❓ Do gift cards expire?
No, they never expire. While the cost of a clean changes over time due to inflation and fuel prices, your gift card can always be credited toward any cleaning service we offer. The value never goes away.
❓ Can I combine multiple gift cards?
Yes! You can combine multiple gift cards toward a single clean or use them across multiple visits. Just let us know when you call or text to schedule.
* About gift card coverage: These gift packages cover most cleans of the size recommended, but not all situations carry the exact cost. A 1,100 sq. ft. home in good condition might require the same effort as a larger seaside manor depending on layout, condition, and specific needs. We charge a fair, straightforward rate every time—if a clean exceeds the card's value, we let you know upfront, and if it's under, you save the difference for next time.
