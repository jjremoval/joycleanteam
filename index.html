FAQ

House Cleaning in San Luis Obispo County — Your Questions, Answered

How much does house cleaning cost in San Luis Obispo County?

Pricing is based on your home's size and how deep a clean it needs. We give you a clear, upfront price before we start — no padded hourly surprises. Call (805) 424-5727 for a free quote.

Will I be charged more if the cleaning takes longer than expected?

No. We quote a flat price based on your home, not an hourly rate. The quote is the price, with no surprises.

Will the price go up once you start the job?

No — the price we quote is the price you pay. We never change rates in the middle of a job or tack on hidden fees. What we quote is what you pay. 👍

Do you offer same-day cleaning?

Yes — same-day and next-day bookings are often available. Call early at (805) 424-5727 and we'll do our best to get to you today.

What's included in a standard cleaning?

Dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchens, bathrooms, and all living spaces. If you want extras like inside the fridge or oven, just ask — we'll quote it up front.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes — we bring all our own supplies and equipment, including eco-friendly, pet-safe products on request. Nothing you need to provide.

Do you do deep cleans or move-out cleans?

Yes — from a standard tidy-up to a full deep clean, including the baseboards, inside cabinets, and other detail work most cleanings skip.

Do you do apartment & student move-out cleanings?

Yes — move-outs are one of our busiest jobs. Whether you're a Cal Poly student clearing out a dorm or apartment or a family moving across town, we get it spotless fast — often same-day — so you get your deposit back. 🎓

Do you offer recurring cleaning plans?

Yes — weekly, biweekly, or monthly maintenance cleanings, with a consistent rate and the same trusted crew whenever possible.

What areas do you serve?

All of San Luis Obispo County — San Luis Obispo, Paso Robles, Atascadero, Arroyo Grande, Pismo Beach, Morro Bay, Nipomo, and nearby towns.

Do I need to be home for the cleaning?

Not always. As long as we have a way in and you've approved the quote, we can often complete the job without you on site.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Joy Clean Team is fully licensed and insured, so your home and property are protected on every job.

How do I get a quote?

Call (805) 424-5727, or use the form at joycleanteam.com. Free, no obligation.
