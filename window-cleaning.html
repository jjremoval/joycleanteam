Window Cleaning FAQ

Window Cleaning Questions — Answered

Do you clean both interior and exterior windows?

Yes — we clean both sides of your windows for a completely streak-free finish. Interior, exterior, screens, and sills — we handle it all.

What's included in your window cleaning service?

Standard window cleaning includes washing glass, frames, screens, and sills. We use professional-grade squeegees and solutions for a sparkling, streak-free finish without water spots.

Do you offer window cleaning for high-rise buildings or hard-to-reach windows?

We handle most residential and commercial window cleaning, including second-story windows with appropriate equipment. Contact us at (805) 457-4584 to discuss your specific needs.

How often should I have my windows cleaned?

For most homes, 2-4 times per year is ideal — typically spring, summer, fall, and after winter. Coastal areas may benefit from more frequent cleaning due to salt spray. We offer flexible recurring schedules.
