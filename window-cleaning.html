Window Cleaning FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Window Cleaning

How much does professional window cleaning cost in San Luis Obispo County?

Window cleaning costs depend on the number of windows, property size, interior vs. exterior service, accessibility, and additional services like screen or track cleaning. We provide personalized free quotes so you know the total cost before scheduling.

Do you clean both interior and exterior windows?

Yes. We clean both sides of your windows for a completely streak-free finish. We also clean window screens, sills, and tracks when requested.

What's included in professional window cleaning?

Standard window cleaning includes washing glass, frames, and sills. We use professional-grade squeegees and eco-friendly solutions. Screen and track cleaning are available as add-ons.

Can you clean hard-water stains from windows?

We can address hard-water deposits and mineral buildup on glass. Some permanent etching may not be fully removable, but we'll assess and explain what's possible during your free quote.

Do you clean second-story windows and hard-to-reach windows?

Yes. We clean second-story and elevated windows using appropriate equipment and safe ground-based methods. Contact us at (805) 457-4584 to discuss your specific situation.

How often should I have my windows professionally cleaned?

For most homes, 2-4 times per year works well. Coastal properties may benefit from more frequent cleaning due to salt spray and marine deposits. We offer flexible monthly, quarterly, and seasonal maintenance schedules.

Do you provide commercial window cleaning for storefronts and offices?

Yes. We provide professional window cleaning for offices, retail storefronts, and commercial properties. Clean glass makes a strong first impression on customers and clients.

Do you specialize in coastal window cleaning for salt spray?

Yes. Coastal salt spray and marine deposits require more frequent cleaning and specialized approaches. We're experienced with coastal property maintenance throughout Pismo Beach, Avila Beach, Shell Beach, and Morro Bay.

Do I need to be home during window cleaning?

It depends on your property and preferences. Contact us to discuss access and your specific situation when scheduling.

How do I schedule a window cleaning appointment?

Call us at (805) 457-4584 or request a free quote through our website. We'll discuss your window cleaning needs and find a time that works for you.
