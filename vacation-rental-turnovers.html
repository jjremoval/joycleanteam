Vacation Rental FAQ

Turnover Cleaning — Your Questions, Answered

How fast can you turn over a vacation rental between guests?

We specialize in rapid turnovers. Most properties are guest-ready in 3–4 hours. For same-day turnovers, call early at (805) 457-4584 and we'll work with you to fit your timeline. We can coordinate arrival, checkout inspections, and turnover cleaning in a single day.

Can you handle multiple turnovers per week?

Yes — vacation rental turnovers are one of our specialties. We work with property managers and individual owners across San Luis Obispo County who have weekly, biweekly, or multiple turnovers per week. We offer recurring scheduling and discount rates for properties with high turnover frequency.

What's included in a vacation rental turnover cleaning?

A complete turnover covers: guest checkout inspection, all surfaces wiped and dusted, bathrooms and kitchens deep-cleaned and sanitized, vacuuming and mopping, trash removed, linens stripped and refreshed, and the property left guest-ready in hours. We can also add specific touches like welcome supplies, welcome notes, or guest-area sanitizing on request.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. We work with individual property owners and professional property management companies across San Luis Obispo County. We can set up automated scheduling, provide recurring discounts, and integrate with your management software.

What if a guest checks out late or I need an emergency turnover?

We understand vacation rental timing is unpredictable. Call us as soon as you know you need a cleaning — we often have same-day or next-morning availability. Our crews are trained for quick turnarounds without cutting corners on quality.

Do you provide Airbnb and Vrbo cleaning services?

Yes. Joy Clean Team provides professional vacation rental cleaning for properties listed on Airbnb, Vrbo, and other booking platforms throughout San Luis Obispo County and the surrounding Central Coast communities.

What is short-term rental (STR) turnover cleaning?

Short-term rental (STR) turnover cleaning is professional cleaning and preparation between guest stays. It ensures the property is thoroughly cleaned, refreshed, and guest-ready for the next arrival — going beyond standard housekeeping to address guest-specific expectations.

What's included in turnover cleaning? What about linens and laundry?

Our standard turnover includes kitchen and bathroom deep cleaning, dusting, vacuuming, mopping, trash removal, and general guest-ready presentation. Linen handling, washing, and bed preparation should be discussed when establishing your cleaning scope, as these services vary by property and agreement. Contact us to clarify what's included for your specific property.

How much does vacation rental or Airbnb cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on property size, condition, cleaning requirements, turnover frequency, and any additional services. We offer competitive rates and recurring discounts for properties with multiple turnovers per week. Contact us for a customized quote based on your property's needs.
